- `sumo-yokozuna.png` — Ōnosato's promotion to the 75th yokozuna on 28 May 2025. Factual reference: Japan Sumo Association, “Onosato Promoted As The 75th Yokozuna In History”: https://sumo.or.jp/EnKyokai/information?id=700
- `suica-mascot-vote.png` — JR East's public-participation process for selecting a new Suica image character. Factual reference: JR East press release, 19 February 2026: https://www.jreast.co.jp/press/2025/20260219_ho01.pdf
- `earthquake-preparedness.png` — the magnitude 7.5 earthquake off Aomori and the emergency response on 8–9 December 2025. Reporting reference: NHK reporting summarized by AP: https://apnews.com/article/493c395f288ff2dc4296db4e7539e5a8
