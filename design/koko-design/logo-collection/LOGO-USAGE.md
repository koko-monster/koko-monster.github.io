## Background guidance

- Use the full-color marks on warm cream, white, or other very light neutral backgrounds.
- On dark purple/navy backgrounds, keep the full-color mark only when contrast is clear; otherwise use a single-color white knockout version.
- On saturated purple, pink, or green panels, use the white knockout version or place the full-color mark inside a light cream panel.
- Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the height of Koko’s ear on all sides.
- Do not stretch, recolor the approved wordmark, add shadows, or place the mark over busy artwork.
- For a fixed small avatar, use `LOGO-002-koko-head.png`; for the design-library favicon, randomly select one of the six approved files in `favicons/` once per reload. For headers, social profiles, and end cards, use `LOGO-001-koko-wordmark.png`.
